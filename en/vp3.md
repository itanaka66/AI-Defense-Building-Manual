## Volumes and Parts

Because the build records are long, this manual is divided into the following three volumes. Part numbers run continuously across volumes.

| Volume | Parts | Main topics |
|---|---|---|
| Volume 1 | Parts 1–23 | Defense foundation (Wazuh, Collector, PostgreSQL, monitoring), AI Defense Manager, a search user for Wazuh, the relay API (Relay), AI analysis and its protection, checks |
| Volume 2 | Parts 24–30 | Enabling and verifying the Wazuh Indexer audit log; checking, recovering and preventing recurrence for the Collector |
| Volume 3 | Parts 31–39 | Handling log switching and recovering missing events, monitoring with Prometheus and testing the rules, checking PostgreSQL, backup and restore testing, the TrueNAS destination |
